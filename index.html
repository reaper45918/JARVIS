Jarvis

Jarvis is a personal, voice-activated assistant that runs on its owner's own Windows PC. It is not a public service: it is used by one person only, its developer.

Privacy policy

What Jarvis accesses. With the owner's permission (Google scope calendar.events.owned), Jarvis creates reminder events in a single Google Calendar named "Jarvis Reminders" that the owner created, and reads back the events it creates to confirm them. It does not read, change, or delete other calendars, contacts, email, or any other Google data.

What is stored. The Google sign-in token and the reminder text stay on the owner's own PC. Reminder events are stored in the owner's Google Calendar, where Google's own privacy policy applies.

What is shared. Nothing. Jarvis does not send Google user data to any other person, company, or server, does not sell it, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models. The assistant's language model runs locally on the owner's PC.

Revoking access. Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party connections.

Contact. Questions: open an issue on github.com/reaper45918/JARVIS.
